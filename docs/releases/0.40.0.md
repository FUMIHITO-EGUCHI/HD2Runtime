# HD2Runtime 0.40.0: enemies and spawning, mission timers, reload/heat/melee, throwables, armor perks, MODS keybinds

0.40.0 is the largest release since 0.30.0. It follows 0.31.3 and absorbs the unreleased 0.32.0 line.

Each item says whether it was seen working in game ("live"). Otherwise it is covered by the offline tests and the
snapshot validators only. Fields that are not live-tested keep `allow_unverified_effect`. Multiplayer behaviour is
claimed only where it says so.

## Enemies, spawning and destruction (offline)

- **Enemy and structure authoring** covers 1,178 entity targets, including 429 structures:
  - creation scale, spawner queues and timing, HiveMind multipliers;
  - transport footprints, shields and jammers;
  - recovered damage-zone names;
  - typed attack chains, with existing names and attack ids preserved.

  See [enemy controls](../enemy-controls.md).
- **Mission difficulty and spawning (`hd2.enemies`)** is edited aboard the ship and affects the next mission:
  - difficulty budgets, all five spawn rosters, composition weights and counts, and breach schedules;
  - zero spawn weight is normal roster suppression, not a global blacklist;
  - spawn compositions are capped at 32 enemies in total after two crash dumps showed the native squad queue
    overflowing. Recipes report the capping, and old presets get the same aggregate check.
- **Demolition templates and destruction models:** shared demolition forces are editable, and all 1,641 destruction
  models can be inspected.
- **Pick your bots/bugs/squids:** spawn ownership is cooperative. Its spawn picks and the Runtime's pending values
  never fight.

## Missions

- **Mission timer durations (`hd2.mission`)** cover 162 mission definitions on 19 shared timer sources. The new
  duration is applied aboard the ship and used when the mission launches.
  - **Live:** the edited value reaches the mission.
  - **The game's limit is 2500 s (41:40), live-proven.** The host clamps its countdown every frame to the replicated
    timer field's range, so a longer value starts at 41:40 in the mission. The writer therefore refuses values above
    2500 s. The ship's mission selector shows the edited definition.
- **Freezing the mission timer (`hd2.freeze_mission_timer`, experimental).** This is the game's own timer pause, the
  one Evacuate High-Value Assets missions spawn with.
  - The countdown stops: no ceiling, and it never reaches zero. Everything the game derives from the countdown waits
    with it. The next mission starts unpaused.
  - **Host only.** The host's freeze is published to the lobby (Runtime member-data key `hd2mt`), and the other
    Runtimes set their own countdown's pause the same way.
  - **Live:** solo freeze and resume. Multiplayer follow is offline only.
  - HVA timers and an expired timer are refused.
- **`hd2.diagnostics.mission_timer()`** is one explicit read. It returns the active instance's timer source, its
  definition, any private record, the countdown, and the paused and expired flags.

## Weapons and equipment (offline)

- **Reload** controls on all 73 reloadable primary and secondary firearms. The Arbitrator, One-Two and Stoker
  underbarrels have their own reload duration (0 = the native ability timing).
- **Heat:**
  - the Double-Edge Sickle: heat, wind-up, level projectile and self-damage, 89 controls;
  - the Variable's RPM slots and the Warrant's modes and burst count;
  - G/SH-39 shield recharge;
  - PLAS-39 charged burst, charge timings and its Single/Unsafe selector.

  See [reload and heat controls](../reload-heat-controls.md).
- **Melee (`hd2.melee`):** 112 weapon and gun-bash entries, covering hit-volume size and offsets, repeat-hit
  intervals, shared damage and stamina costs. See [knife and melee authoring](../knife-melee.md).
- **Throwables:**
  - eleven launch/spin controls on all 23 throwables, including the K-2 knife's launch curve and speed override;
  - four status types and strengths on main explosions, knife hits, shrapnel and Pineapple bomblets;
  - arming delay for 22 explosives.

  See [throwable statuses](../throwable-statuses.md). Knife explosions remain unavailable.
- **Explosion-created arcs:** the GL-52 De-Escalator and G-31 Arc expose range, velocity, spread, chain and split
  limits, arc damage and stun. The G-31 also gets four arc-hit status slots. See [explosion arcs](../explosion-arcs.md).
- **Projectile swaps:**
  - the GL-28's backpack-fed projectile swap, shotgun donors included;
  - the G-109 Urchin's pulse payload override (small or charged Epoch plasma);
  - the W.A.S.P.'s hit projectile for both missile modes. The missile entity keeps its own flight.
- **Supply Pack:** box capacity, starting boxes and refill, plus the shared stim refill amount.
- **Armor perks:** the two equipped passives move onto the Helldiver. Armor perks is its own catalogue, with 75
  numeric controls across 31 perks.
- **Hoverpack:** the landing-thrust labels are corrected; offsets and ids are unchanged.

## Input: the game's own keybind page (live)

- **Native keybinds:** `hd2.input.bind(id, {key, on_press, native = {label, category}})` also registers the binding with
  **Mod Bindings Menu** (CowboyBingus), on the game's Controls page, MODS tab. Any key, mouse button or controller
  input bound there presses it.
- **One key, not two:** while a keyboard or mouse input is set there, the binding's own key is ignored. Emptying it
  brings the key back immediately.
  - The Runtime reads the game's live binding table, read only and pinned, to know this.
  - A controller-only binding leaves the key working.
- **HD2Runtime's settings window (F10) and the HD2R Editor** declare it. Labels follow the language. See
  [events](../events.md).

## Custom stratagems and multiplayer

- **Carrier slots never move while the lobby is waiting** for a member's value, for example after a mission. A move
  on that partial allocation was undone too late and left a slot desynced and locked. In one report, the host's
  Pelican Gatling was shown as a locked Orbital Napalm Barrage. Offline; reproduced and fixed by test.
- **Pelican variants:** the Autocannon Pelican keeps its native burst AI. A transaction that returns `ALREADY_DESIRED`
  is accepted as success.

## Runtime and performance

- **F10 Performance:** Global and Runtime views with exclusive core/mod accounting, an on-demand tracked-memory graph,
  editor UI/work attribution, and a watchdog that names the external mod behind a slow frame.
- **Faster application:** large presets apply faster. Discovery walks are shared between the weapon and enemy writers
  (a reported preset of 554 operations had taken 77 s). Settled ensures sleep between their periodic checks.
- **Mission cleanup fix:** mission-scoped timers and subscriptions are now cleared when a mission ends even if no mod
  listens to `mission_ended`. Before, they stayed active.
- **SDK fix:** the authoring stub declared `hd2.passives.perk` on a table it never created.
- **Translations:** new texts in all 13 languages. The Korean F10 peak label avoids a glyph the game's font lacks.

## Known

- One tester reported a crash when opening F10. No cause was found and it has not recurred. The F10 panel keeps its
  sparse diagnostic breadcrumbs.
- **GalacticMenuHotkey (not HD2Runtime):** its Galactic Map shortcut sits on Mod Bindings Menu's fixed slot 1, which
  inherits left click and A/Cross. On the war table every click re-opens the map. Rebind or disable that shortcut.
- Not live-tested: everything not marked live above. That includes the multiplayer timer follow, the
  waiting-lobby carrier fix and most new fields.
